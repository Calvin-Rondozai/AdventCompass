A Seventh-day Adventist companion

AdventCompass

Scripture, hymnal, and the writings of Ellen G. White, held together by an AI assistant that only knows what's already on your shelf. Twenty-eight features, one phone, no connection required.

Friday cuts over to Sabbath at a fixed 5:00 PM. Saturday's end is a real astronomical sunset, recalculated every week from your GPS location — never a fixed clock.
